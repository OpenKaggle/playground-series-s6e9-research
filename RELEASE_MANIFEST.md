# Release manifest

## Included

- user-authored training, audit, ensemble, and submission-validation scripts;
- compact aggregate validation reports and experiment ledger;
- official access and submission receipts;
- source audit reports that cite, rather than copy, third-party notebooks.

## Excluded

- `data/`, `external/`, `artifacts/`, `submissions/`, and `public_notebooks/`;
- raw predictions, OOF rows, trained models, downloaded datasets, and caches;
- credentials, virtual environments, and machine-local metadata.

The public archive is meant to document methodology. It does not grant access
to any competition data and is not a leaderboard-submission bundle.
